# Workspaces built by AI

An AI agent can set up a complete ROUT workspace for you — profile design and "Login with ROUT" apps included — without you doing anything first. Once everything is ready, you claim the workspace and become its owner.

## What the AI can do on its own

- Create a workspace with a name, address and description.
- Design a profile in the Studio: name, tagline, theme, card style, blocks and display settings.
- Prepare up to three login apps with their redirect addresses.

Until someone claims it, nothing is public: no profile, no working login app, no e-mail. An unclaimed workspace is fully deleted after 48 hours.

## How to claim

The AI gives you a **claim link**, a **QR code** or a **claim code** (`ROUT-XXXXX-…`):

1. Open the link, **or**
2. Go to **Profile → Personal** and tap the **gear** next to **New workspace**.
3. Choose **Claim workspace**, scan the QR code or type the code.
4. Sign in and confirm. You become owner; the login apps go live and you see their secrets once.

## Security

- Link, code and sandbox key are random and stored only as hashes.
- Every claim works once. After the claim the AI loses its key.
- The AI never receives your e-mail address or other data.
- The profile only goes live when you publish it.

## For AI agents

Machine-readable steps: [/.well-known/rout-agent.json](/.well-known/rout-agent.json) and [llms.txt](/llms.txt). In short: `GET /api/agent/sandbox` → solve the proof-of-work → `POST /api/agent/sandbox` → `POST /api/agent/sandbox/workspace` → `PATCH /api/agent/sandbox/profile` → `PUT /api/agent/sandbox/apps` → hand the claim link, QR code or code to your human.
