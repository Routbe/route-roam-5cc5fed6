# ROUT

ROUT is your sovereign digital identity. One place for your profiles, your wallet, your physical card and secure sign-in across the web. You decide what is public; everything else stays encrypted.

## Four building blocks

- **Profiles.** A public profile card at `rout.be/@yourname`, plus anonymous aliases kept apart from your real name. See [the platform](/about/platform).
- **Sovereign wallet.** Three passes in Apple or Google Wallet: Public Node, Stealth Alias and Sovereign Key. See [Wallet](/info/wallet).
- **Metal card with dual scan.** The printed QR shows only your public profile; real access runs via NFC or a rotating code. See [the ROUT Card](/about/rout-card).
- **Universal sign-in.** Sign in anywhere with ROUT; each app gets its own code and never a list of your other accounts. See [connected apps](/info/connected-apps).
