# ROUT platform

Identity, profiles, QR codes and connected services.

## Identity and consent

ROUT ID uses OpenID Connect and PKCE. Apps request scoped claims; users grant consent. Human verification and legal identity verification are different signals.

## Profiles and publishing

Root profiles, privacy aliases and workspaces are separate presentation spaces. Studio edits private drafts; publication is explicit and revision-checked.

## QR codes, links and analytics

Static QR codes hold their destination directly. Dynamic destinations require account configuration. Available click and visit statistics must be minimized and scoped to the owner.

## Cards and direct support

The ROUT Card separates public QR, NFC verification and owner-only rotating tokens. Support payments go to the recipient’s bank or configured payment provider, not a ROUT intermediary account.

## Optional integrations

Google Wallet, email, payments, music and social services each require provider configuration. Availability is not a promise of universal access or certification.
