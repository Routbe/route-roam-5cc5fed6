# The ROUT card

The ROUT card is your secure card for **payments and access**. Each side has its own job, and it sits next to two ordinary profile cards. Only **verified members** can create cards.

## Two sides, two jobs

| Side | What | Who may scan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Front — large QR** | Opens the profile card you chose: public profile or alias profile | Anyone. Never grants access, cannot pay |
| **Back — small QR + NFC** | Your secure payment and access code | Only a verified reader or till |

For your public profile, the large QR ends in six digits (`rout.be/<name>/123456`) that change **every 10 minutes**, so a forwarded photo of your card goes stale quickly. A partner (pool, transport) can ask whether the code is still fresh; visitors simply land on your profile.

The small back QR is a long, single-use secure code that expires **every minute**. It never links to a profile.

## Choose where the front leads

In the ROUT card screen, pick **Public profile** or **Alias profile** under **Large front QR**. Note: while the alias is selected, anyone scanning your card can see that this alias belongs to you.

## Three cards in your Wallet

| Card | Purpose | NFC | QR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ROUT card** (Sovereign Key) | Payments and access | Yes | Google Wallet: a QR that changes every minute and works once. Apple Wallet: no QR, NFC only |
| **Public profile card** | Your business card | No | QR to your public profile |
| **Alias profile card** | Business card for your alias | No | QR to your alias profile |

The ROUT card in your Wallet carries no profile data. Profile cards never carry NFC and can never pay. More: [Wallet](/info/wallet).

<Callout type="info" title="Pending approval">The secure ROUT card in Google Wallet and Apple Wallet becomes available once Google (Smart Tap) and Apple approve the NFC feature. The physical card follows later. Profile cards work independently.</Callout>

## Card number and privacy

The card number is derived with a secret key. It does not reveal your internal account ID, and cards for your main profile and aliases cannot be linked. The serial stays the same when you change your username.

## Lost it? Freeze it

One button freezes the card. All QR and live codes stop working until you unfreeze it. A finder cannot pay with it or see whose it is, beyond the profile you share publicly.
