# Brand and design

Recognizable ROUT artwork without misleading claims.

## Official assets

Use the official ROUT rabbit and wordmark. Keep proportions and sufficient clear space. Public artwork must not contain account secrets or access tokens.

## Press collections

The [press kit](/press) contains brand assets. [Wallet artwork](/press/wallet) provides generic PNGs; [share previews](/press/previews) provides localized page images.

## Accessible presentation

Preserve readable contrast, keyboard access and reduced-motion behavior. Decorative effects must not prevent people from reading content or using controls.
