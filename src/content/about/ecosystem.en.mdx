# Ecosystem

Separate responsibilities for identity, data and providers.

## Infrastructure boundaries

ROUT handles profiles, QR and consent. Neon stores relational data; object storage holds files. Hosting runs the app. Each provider’s region, contract and subprocessors must be checked for the actual deployment.

## ROUT and delplanche.cloud

The shared vision favors open protocols and independent hosting. This relationship does not prove that every underlying supplier has identical legal or technical properties.

## Connections are optional

Connected services retain their own terms. Linking an account does not automatically publish its data. Review consent and visibility before enabling a connection.
