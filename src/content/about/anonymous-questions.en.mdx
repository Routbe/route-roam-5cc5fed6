# Anonymous questions

No sender name shown to the recipient is not absolute anonymity.

## Owner control

Choose the allowed audience and whether to accept questions. A received question is not automatically public; the owner chooses which answer to publish.

## Safety and limits

Abuse controls and operational records can still exist. Text, context and timing can reveal identity. Never promise untraceability.

## Optional assistance

Ghost Mode can help rephrase a question with explicit use of an AI provider. Review suggestions yourself. See [responsible AI](/about/responsible-ai).
