# Responsible AI

Optional assistance, explicit review and honest privacy boundaries.

## Human decisions

AI suggestions do not automatically speak for the owner. Review and edit them before sending or publishing. They can be inaccurate.

## Data sent to providers

Using an AI feature can send the supplied text to its configured provider. Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal information. Optional integrations need clear consent.

## Not an anonymity guarantee

Rewriting can change tone, but context or specific details may still identify someone. No promise of undetectability, legal compliance or perfect safety.
