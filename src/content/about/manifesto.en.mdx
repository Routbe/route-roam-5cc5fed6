# Manifesto

People are not products.

## Software serves people

ROUT rejects advertising trackers and trading in behavioral profiles. Functional sessions are still needed for account sign-in.

## Identity is not an email address

OpenID Connect and pairwise identifiers limit cross-app reuse. Consent is not a blank cheque; applications must validate tokens and link accounts carefully.

## Privacy is not magic

Standard sign-in is visible to the identity service. Encryption of stored ledger content does not make operational infrastructure blind.

## Open source and honest limits

AGPL-3.0-or-later supports inspection and self-hosting, subject to its license terms. Self-hosting still requires updates, key management, backups and incident response. No compliance, post-quantum or perfect-security claims without evidence.
