# Sovereignty

Control includes the ability to change providers.

## Open protocols

OpenID Connect makes app requests and shared claims explicit. Public profiles and custom domains remain subject to the visibility choices of their owners.

## Source and operations

AGPL source access supports inspection and migration. Separate database, storage and hosting responsibilities reduce lock-in but still require secure credentials and backups.

## No absolute independence

Domain registrars, hosting and optional login providers are dependencies. A European region alone does not prove compliance or complete independence.
