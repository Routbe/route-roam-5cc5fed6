# Names & domains

A name helps visitors find you. A personal name, a workspace, and a custom domain have different owners and responsibilities.

## Personal: @name

A personal handle belongs to your account. `@name` is the recognizable format; your public profile uses the corresponding web address. Check availability and any necessary approval before putting a name on printed materials.

## Business: w/company

A workspace represents an organization. `w/company` makes that context visible. Access and management belong to the workspace and its authorized members, not automatically to every personal account linked to it.

## Custom domain

A custom domain can point to your profile once domain verification and routing are set up. ROUT requires proof of ownership via the specified DNS steps. Owning a domain is not proof of a verified person or business registration.

| Choice | Context | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| @name | Personal identity | Handle and profile visibility |
| w/company | Organization | Ownership and member permissions |
| Custom domain | Custom web address | DNS verification, certificate, and routing |

## Changes without surprises

Changing a handle or domain can impact existing links, QR codes, and external bios. Update those references and don't rely on a permanent redirect without testing it yourself. Verification based on an external bio must match again after a name change.
