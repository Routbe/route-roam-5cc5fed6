# Tips and support

ROUT never moves money itself. By default, a tip page displays a SEPA QR code (EPC) and your IBAN as text, so visitors pay directly through their own bank.

## What you need

- A verified account with your own primary name.
- An IBAN under a name that matches your verified (or approved business) name.

## Optional payment providers

Want card payments? Connect your own Stripe or Mollie account. For Stripe, we only accept restricted keys. Keys are stored encrypted and only used during a payment.

## What ROUT does not do

- No intermediary account and no commission on your tips.
- No storage of your visitors' payment details.
