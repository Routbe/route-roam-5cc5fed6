# Custom badges

An issuer's badge is not an official ROUT identity verification.

<Callout type="warning" title="Not the same as an official checkmark">
A custom badge is a statement made by the issuer. ROUT identity verification, accreditation, and professional qualification are separate matters.
</Callout>

## The issuer remains visible

A badge next to a name opens a details window. There, you can see the title, the description, and the app that issued the badge. If the app is linked to a public ROUT profile, you can visit the issuer.

A logo or a well-known name does not prove a relationship with a school, company, or government authority on its own. Check the issuer's profile and criteria before using a badge to make a decision.

## You control publication

The credential extension tracks acceptance, app visibility, and display next to the name separately. A hidden app, a revoked credential, or revoked app permissions must not be displayed publicly. A private profile also does not make credentials public.

The existing public view supports these boundaries. External issuance and the controls to accept new credentials are not yet a live verified service; this page does not claim that every app can already send badges.

## A badge is not a diploma

A badge can describe membership or participation. A certificate or diploma describes a different claim. None of those labels automatically makes the content valid, up to date, or cryptographically verifiable.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the badge">Read the full title and description, not just the icon.</Step>
<Step title="Check the issuer">Check the app and the linked ROUT profile. Watch out for names that imitate an official authority.</Step>
<Step title="Verify the claim">If necessary, ask the issuer for criteria, validity, and the original proof.</Step>
</StepByStep>
