# Bookings

Bookings gives you your own booking page on your profile. Visitors only see the moments when you are free, pick a day and time in their own time zone and receive an invitation right away.

## How it works

1. Open your agenda in ROUT and create a meeting type: length, place (video, address or phone) and whether you confirm every request yourself first.
2. Set your working hours, with exceptions for holidays or a day off. Optionally connect your own calendar through a calendar link (ICS) or CalDAV.
3. Share your link or show your next free times on your profile.

## What visitors see

- A month calendar with the days that still have room, and the free times grouped by morning, afternoon and evening.
- Times in the visitor's own time zone, in 12- or 24-hour format. Visitors can choose another time zone.
- As soon as a visitor picks a time, it stays reserved for 5 minutes while they fill in their details.

## Your rules

- Breaks before and after every meeting, and a minimum notice within which nobody can book any more.
- How far ahead people can book, also counted in working days.
- A maximum number of meetings per day, week or month, and per visitor.
- Only show times right next to another meeting, so your day does not fall apart into fragments.

## Groups and teams

A group session has a fixed number of seats: several people book the same time until it is full. In a workspace a meeting can go to one fixed team member, to whoever is free first, or to all team members together.

## Privacy

- From connected calendars ROUT only keeps when you are busy.
- Answers to questions you mark as sensitive are stored encrypted.
- Every booking is checked at the moment it is made, so nobody gets double-booked.

## Prefer your own calendar service?

You can also show a card with a Cal.com or Calendly booking link on your profile. That service then handles the meetings and the confirmation.
