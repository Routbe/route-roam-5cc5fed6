# Payment pages

A payment page is a calm place where fans, customers, or friends can send you something, similar to Buy Me a Coffee, but without a platform sitting between you and your money.

## Always available: SEPA QR

Your page displays an EPC/SEPA QR and your IBAN as text. A visitor scans it with their banking app and confirms. No account needed, no fees from ROUT.

## Optional: card payments via your own account

Connect your own Stripe or Mollie account. ROUT stores the key encrypted and only decrypts it at the moment of payment. Stripe only accepts restricted keys, so a leaked key never exposes your entire account.

## What you need

A verified account with a primary profile. The account holder's name must match your confirmed legal or approved business name.
