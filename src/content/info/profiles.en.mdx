# Profile Hub

Your Profile Hub brings your links, linked accounts, and selected information together. Not everything you connect needs to be visible to visitors.

## Building your profile

Start with your name, photo, and the links you want to share. Use Studio to organize elements and then check the public view. Publishing and saving an edit are different steps: always check what a visitor actually sees.

## Connections and audiences

Linked accounts can target a professional, social, or combined audience. Choose for each connection whether it appears on your profile. A self-entered username is not the same as an identity verified by a provider.

## Last.fm, Chess, and Steam

Music and gaming connections add context to your profile. Last.fm can display a current track; Steam can provide a live card if the required service is configured. A Chess reference links to your chess identity. Availability varies by integration: a link, a public card, and a verified connection do not mean the same thing.

<Callout type="info" title="You choose what appears">A linked account does not grant general permission to make all data from that provider public. Check the visibility of each element.</Callout>

## Badges and credentials

A ROUT identity marker is separate from a badge or certificate from a third-party issuer. The [official badges](/info/badges), [custom badges](/info/custom-badges), and [credentials](/info/credentials) each carry their own meaning.

## Before you share

Check your profile while signed out. Make sure your photo, links, and audience choices are correct, and verify that hidden elements are truly omitted. Then use your profile URL or a [QR code](/info/qr-studio).
