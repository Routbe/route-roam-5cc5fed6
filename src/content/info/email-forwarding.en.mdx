# Email forwarding

With forwarding, you get an address in the form of `jouwnaam@rout.be`. Messages sent to that address arrive in your existing mailbox. ROUT does not keep a copy as a mailbox.

## Who can use it

Forwarding is available for verified members, Early Believers, and paying members. Without that status, you will see the section, but it will be locked.

## Setting up

1. Open the email forwarding section in your dashboard.
2. Enter the address where you want to receive messages.
3. Confirm via the link in the email you receive. Forwarding only starts after that.

If the confirmation email doesn't arrive, the dashboard displays the confirmation link directly.

## Displaying publicly

You choose whether `jouwnaam@rout.be` is displayed on your profile. Your real email address is never shown.
