# Using anonymous questions

Receive questions on your profile or via a separate question page. You decide who can write to you and which answers you publish.

## Turn on receiving questions

Open [Studio](/studio) for the profile you want to customize. Receiving questions is disabled by default. Under the question settings, turn on **Accept messages** and choose your target audience:

- **Everyone**: visitors can write without a ROUT account, unless stricter restrictions apply.
- **ROUT members only**: the sender must be logged in.
- **Verified ROUT members only**: a logged-in, verified account is required.

Even if an account is required, account details are never shown to the recipient. Read more about the boundaries in [Anonymity & safety](/safety/anonymous-questions).

## Profile block or separate question page

A question block appears on the profile hub where you add it. Want a standalone link? Then enable the **Anonymous question page** under **Settings** in Studio; on that page, you can only customize the design, plus choose whether your profile picture and a button to your main profile are displayed. A personal profile uses `/<naam>/ask`; a privacy alias uses `/u/<alias>/ask`.

The separate page offers settings for the design, the profile picture, and a link to the main profile. It is not a second fully customizable profile hub. The question form and answers can be styled independently.

## Saving is not publishing

**Save** stores your Studio draft. Only **Publish** makes the updated profile settings and design public. Afterward, check the question link for the correct profile. Choices for your personal profile do not automatically change those of your privacy alias.

## Sending a message

1. Open the recipient's question block or question link.
2. Write a message of up to **300 characters**. The dice gives you an idea; it doesn't send anything.
3. Optionally use [Ghost Mode](/info/ghost-mode) and review the suggestion.
4. Select **Send anonymously** when you're satisfied.

Do not send names, contact details, or personal information you don't want to share. A hidden sender name does not make recognizable content unrecognizable.

## If sending fails

A closed page does not accept questions. A restricted audience may require you to log in or be verified. A maximum of **five messages per minute** and **twenty per hour per IP address** applies; on a shared network, you share these limits with other visitors.

A delivery confirmation is not a read receipt and does not guarantee an answer. Messages from muted or temporarily blocked senders may receive the same confirmation without ever appearing in the inbox.

## Questions and answers are not a guestbook

The [guestbook](/info/guestbook) is a separate feature for notes from verified members. Anonymous questions have their own inbox and only become public when explicitly published along with an answer.
