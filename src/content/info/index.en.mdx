# Using ROUT

Your name, your links, your identity. You decide what you show and what you share.

## Make your profile recognizable

The [Profile Hub](/info/profiles) brings your links and accounts together. [Names & domains](/info/usernames) distinguishes your personal, business, and domain identity.

## Understand what you see

[Official badges](/info/badges) provide specific signals. A [custom badge](/info/custom-badges) comes from an individual issuer and is not a ROUT verification.

## QR codes and access

Use [QR Studio](/info/qr-studio) for your codes. A [Wallet](/info/wallet) pass is not automatically proof of identity.

Receive [tips](/info/tips), let visitors schedule [bookings](/info/bookings), or open your [guestbook](/info/guestbook).

## Anonymous questions and answers

Read [Using anonymous questions](/info/anonymous-questions) to turn on receiving and choose your audience. [Answers & stories](/info/answers-and-stories) covers your inbox and sharing; [Ghost Mode](/info/ghost-mode) explains optional rewriting. Make sure to check the [limits of anonymity](/safety/anonymous-questions) beforehand as well.
