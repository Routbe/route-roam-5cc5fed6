# Links and analytics

ROUT does what you know from Bitly, QR Code Generator, and QRCodeMonkey, but from Europe and without tracking visitors.

## Short links

Create a short link with a custom alias, on rout.be or your own domain. Change the destination whenever you want; the link stays the same.

## Dynamic and static QR codes

A static code contains the content directly and works forever without ROUT. A dynamic code points to a short link, so you don't need to reprint your materials when the destination changes. Customize shape, colors, frames, and logos in the [QR-studio](/info/qr-studio) or generate thousands of codes at once.

## Analytics without cookies

See how often your links and codes are used, without cookies or profiling visitors.
