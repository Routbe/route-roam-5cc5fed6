# Ghost Mode: Neutralising Your Writing Style

Your choice of words can give away who you are. Ghost Mode gives you a more neutral version of your message on request, which you can review yourself first.

## How rewriting works

1. Write your question or message.
2. Select the sparkle icon next to the message field.
3. Read the suggestion that appears in the same field. Check meaning, tone, and personal details.
4. Edit it or choose **Restore original** to bring back your text from before the rewrite.
5. Send only when you want to.

Rewriting is optional. Restoring the original sends nothing. The recipient may see a style tag on a rewritten message; that is not proof that the text is unrecognizable.

## The same language, not necessarily the same nuance

The AI is instructed to preserve the core message and stay in the same language. Slang, dialect, typos, excessive emojis, and distinctive writing habits can be made more neutral. With very short or ambiguous text, the site language may serve as a hint.

A model can change meaning or misjudge the language. The simple filter understands less context. So always check a suggestion, even if it sounds good.

## What gets processed

By triggering a rewrite, you submit the entered text to be processed by the ROUT rewrite feature and, when available, the configured external AI service. Infomaniak AI Tools is the default setup; a different service can be configured. The request contains the text, rewriting instructions, and if necessary a language hint, not your complete inbox or ROUT profile.

The original remains available in the form for restoration and is not saved as a second question in the inbox. The ROUT rewrite feature does not log the message text. Do not take this as a general promise regarding the processing or retention periods of an external service.

## When the service does not reply

A simple rule-based filter may be used when no usable model response is available. The notice on your message will make this clear. If the request fails completely, your text stays unchanged; you can continue writing yourself or try again later.

Limits of up to **three rewrites per minute** and **twenty per hour per IP address** apply. Rewriting and sending messages have separate counters.

## What Ghost Mode cannot promise

Ghost Mode does not hide your identity, does not reliably strip all personal data, and does not guarantee anonymity. It does not answer your question and does not endorse a message as safe. Do not put sensitive data into the text expecting AI to remove it afterwards.
