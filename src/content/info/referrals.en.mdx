# Invite friends

Every member has a personal invite link. Anyone who signs up through that link counts towards your milestones.

## Your link

Verified members share a link with their own name, such as `rout.be/r/jouwnaam`. Free members share a link with their alias, so their name isn't visible.

## Milestones

| Sign-ups | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 3 friends | 50% discount |
| 3 verified friends | Free |
| 10 friends | Free and the Influencer badge |

You can see in your dashboard how many friends have signed up and what your next milestone is.

## Privacy

You only see numbers, not a list of who has signed up.
