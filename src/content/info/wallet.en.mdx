# Wallet

Under **Wallet** in your ROUT account you will find three separate cards. They are deliberately apart: your business card and your payment key never share a pass.

<Callout type="info" title="Verified members only">Without a verified profile you cannot add wallet cards.</Callout>

## The three cards

| Card | For | NFC | QR code | Profile data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ROUT card** (Sovereign Key) | Payments and access | Yes | Google Wallet: changes every minute and works once. Apple Wallet: no QR | None |
| **Public profile card** | Your business card | No | To your public profile | Your public name and colour |
| **Alias profile card** | Business card for your alias | No | To your alias profile | Only the alias |

### Adding them

- Click **ROUT card → Add to Wallet** to get the secure payment pass. In Google Wallet it works with NFC and a QR that renews every minute. In Apple Wallet it works with NFC only, because Apple does not allow a changing QR.
- Click **Profile cards** to get a pass with the QR of your public profile or of your alias.
- No alias yet? Create one first to make the alias card.

## Secure QR or profile QR?

- A **profile QR** simply opens a web page. Anyone may scan it.
- The **secure QR** of the ROUT card is not a web address. Only a ROUT reader or till can check it. Each code works once and expires after a minute, so a photo of it quickly becomes worthless.

On your physical or 3D ROUT card, the large front QR leads to the profile card you choose; the small back QR is the secure code. See [The ROUT card](/about/rout-card).

## When is it available?

Profile cards work once Apple and Google issuing is set up for ROUT. The secure ROUT card is still waiting for NFC approval from Google (Smart Tap) and Apple; until then your Wallet screen shows **Not available yet**.

## Lost, changed or removed

- **Lost your phone or card?** Freeze your ROUT card in your account. All codes stop working immediately.
- **Removing a pass from your Wallet app** only removes that copy. Your ROUT account and cards in ROUT remain.
- A saved pass may show an older state, so every scan always checks the current status with ROUT.
