# Replies & stories

You manage your anonymous inbox. Receiving a message doesn't obligate you to reply or make anything public.

## From inbox to public reply

Open the anonymous inbox for the right profile. Choose **Reply via ROUT**, write your reply, and check both the question and your own text. A reply can contain up to **1,000 characters**.

With **Publish to profile**, you make the question and the reply public. The visitor won't see any ROUT sender details, but they will see the content of the question. So don't share a sensitive message just because the sender's name is hidden.

## The 24-hour status ring

Published replies appear for **24 hours** as a status around your profile picture. A visitor can swipe through the replies in the story view. The expiration of the status ring doesn't automatically remove the published reply from your profile.

## Sharing as a story card

Choose **Share on socials** to create a question sticker or a question-with-reply card. You can download a card, copy the link, or use the available sharing options. The image is in the vertical story format **1080 × 1920**.

For Instagram or Snapchat, you post the image to your story yourself and add the question link using a link sticker. A downloaded image doesn't contain an automatically clickable link and won't be posted to your social account automatically.

<Callout type="info" title="A shared copy remains">A card or screenshot that someone has already saved won't disappear when you later hide or delete the reply on ROUT.</Callout>

## Updating, hiding, and deleting

- **Update reply** edits your published reply.
- **Remove from profile** stops public publication on ROUT.
- **Delete** removes the message from your inbox.
- **Mute this sender** prevents new inbox messages from the recognized sender without revealing their identity.

Muting and deleting are different actions. A mute remains in place until you lift it yourself under **Muted senders**. Read the [safety limits](/safety/anonymous-questions), including what sender recognition can and cannot do.

## No private chat back to the sender

This reply feature publishes an answer on your profile. It is not a direct private conversation with the anonymous sender. Don't share passwords, contact details, or other private information in a reply.
