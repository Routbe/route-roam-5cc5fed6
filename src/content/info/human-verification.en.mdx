# Confirming human or name

ROUT has two levels of confirmation. You choose what you want checked.

## Two levels

| Level | What it proves | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real human | You are one unique person, without revealing your name | A verification service or manual review |
| Legal identity | Your legal name, from an official document | Passport or eID, for example via itsme |

Legal verification automatically confirms that you are a real human as well.

## What is publicly visible

- **Main profile** — after legal verification, the blue checkmark and your legal name.
- **Alias** — never your legal name. You can show a "real human" badge if you turn that on yourself.

## Where else it is used

Anyone creating a tip page must be verified, and the account holder must match the confirmed name. This prevents anyone from redirecting payments through a free alias.
