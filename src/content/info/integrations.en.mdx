# Integrations

In the Connections Hub, you link the places where you're active. For each connection, you choose whether it appears on your professional, social, or both profiles.

## What you can connect

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Music and video | Spotify (now listening), YouTube channel card |
| Social | Bluesky, Mastodon, Snapchat, Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Discord, Twitch |
| Code and work | GitHub, GitLab, WakaTime |
| Gaming and learning | Chess.com, Lichess, Duolingo |
| Publishing and payments | RSS, Lightning, Monero, Ethereum |

## Honest stats

Your total reach only counts accounts you've genuinely connected: open platforms are fetched automatically, while others are entered per connection. No estimated or inflated numbers.

## Privacy

Tokens are stored encrypted on the server and are never sent to the browser. Visitors view cached cards, so their IP address doesn't end up with the third-party service. Disconnecting revokes access and deletes the data.
