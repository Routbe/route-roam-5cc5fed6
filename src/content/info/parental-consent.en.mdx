# Parental consent under 16

Are you under 16? ROUT will ask for consent from a parent or guardian before your account fully works. That way, we comply with European privacy regulations (GDPR) and keep young members safe.

## How it works

1. When creating your account, enter your date of birth.
2. If you're under 16, ROUT asks for the email address of your parent or guardian.
3. Your parent receives an email with a personal, one-time link.
4. Using that link, your parent gives consent and chooses who can send you messages.
5. After that, your account works. Your parent can update the settings later using the same link.

## What your parent can manage

- **Consent**: without consent, your account remains limited.
- **Who can send messages**: off, verified accounts only, all ROUT members, or everyone. By default, this is set to verified accounts only.
- **Payments**: if you want to buy something, the request goes to your parent. They have 48 hours to pay; otherwise, it expires.

## Privacy for you and your parent

- The link in the email is your parent's key. ROUT only stores an encrypted fingerprint of it, never the link itself.
- Your parent cannot see your messages, replies, or visitors.
- We only use your parent's email address for consent and payments, never for marketing.
- When you turn 16, parental control expires automatically.
