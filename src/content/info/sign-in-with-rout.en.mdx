# Sign in with ROUT

Your ROUT ID is a single account you use to sign in to both ROUT and other apps.

## Passwordless

Passkeys rely on your device's security: no passwords that can leak or be phished. For irreversible changes, such as merging accounts or modifying 2FA, ROUT asks for a one-time six-digit code via email.

## Signing in to other apps

Apps and partners use the open standard OpenID Connect. The first time, you see which data an app requests and you choose what you share. An app never receives more than what you allow.

## Other ways to sign in

You can also connect Google, Apple, Bluesky, Mastodon, and other accounts. Accounts without an email address verified by the provider are only linked after entering an email code, ensuring no one can type in your address to take over your account.
