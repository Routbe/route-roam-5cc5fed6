# Connected apps

When you sign in somewhere with ROUT, you decide what that app gets. You see it upfront on the consent screen and can revoke it later.

## What an app can see

- Its own code for you, valid only at that app.
- Your name and profile photo, if you allow it.
- The email address you pick: your real address or a private relay.
- Extra proof, like "this is a real human", only if you approve it separately later.

## What an app never sees

- Your other accounts, such as Google or GitHub.
- Which other apps you use.
- Your payment keys, documents or anonymous aliases.
- Your code at another app, so two apps can't link you together.

## Staying in control

In Account & Security you see which apps are connected and what they received. You can disconnect an app, or pass a new email address only to the apps you tick. See also [why ROUT never shares lists](/info/no-account-lists).
