# Newsletter sign-up

With the sign-up block, visitors can leave their email address on your profile.

## Adding it

Add the sign-up block to your profile in the editor. In the block's settings, you can see how many people have signed up and how many of them have confirmed their address.

## Connect with Brevo

If you use Brevo, you can connect a list. New sign-ups will then also be sent to that list.

## Downloading addresses

Only you can download the list as a CSV file. By default, you only get confirmed addresses. The addresses are stored encrypted and are only decrypted for this download. You cannot download an unlimited number of times in a row.

## Unsubscribing

Every recipient can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the email.
