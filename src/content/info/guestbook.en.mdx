# Guestbook

With the guestbook, other members can leave a short note on your profile.

## Who can write

Only logged-in, verified members who are not blocked. This keeps spam away.

## What visitors see

Only messages that you pin, and only if your profile is public.

## Management

You can pin or delete any message on your profile.
