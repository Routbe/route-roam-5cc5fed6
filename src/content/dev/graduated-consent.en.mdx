# Graduated consent

Don't ask for everything at first sign-in. Start small and ask for more at the moment you truly need it. The consent screen then shows only what is new.

## Step 1: plain sign-in

Start with `openid`, optionally `profile` and `email`. You receive a `sub` that only applies to your app.

## Step 2: verify with ROUT when needed

Later, send the member to `/oauth/authorize` again with the extra scope. The member only sees the new request.

```text
# Day 1: plain sign-in
scope=openid profile email

# Later, at checkout: "Verify with ROUT"
scope=openid sybil:verify
```

| Scope | What you get | Requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sybil:verify` | Proof this is one real human | Tier 1 or 2 |
| `identity:legal_name` | Legal name | Tier 2 |
| `identity:national_id` | National id number | Tier 2, trusted KYC partner and a fresh step-up |

## When the member isn't verified yet

If the member lacks the required tier, the claim is simply missing. Handle that gracefully: explain and let the member try again later. For `identity:national_id` you otherwise get a 403 with `tier_2_required` or `step_up_required`.

## Good habits

- Request a sensitive scope only at the action that needs it.
- Store only the result you need, not the whole token.
- Every sensitive disclosure is logged, so members can see who received what.
